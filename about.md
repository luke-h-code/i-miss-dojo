# About

In 2019 Dojo shut down and a huge part of my life went away.

[Dojo](https://www.businessinsider.com/dojo-app-raises-800k-funding-2015-1) started as an app, then became a website and newsletter, and gave the best advice on what to do and where to visit in London. It gave me some of the best weekends and dates I've ever had. Even when everything else sucked, I knew I'd at least have a great weekend.

It's been gone for years, and [nothing has really filled its shoes](https://www.reddit.com/r/london/comments/cseq3z/any_alternatives_to_dojo/). Now that AI is available, I figured I could at least partly bring back that great advice. I took 20+ of the newsletters I'd received, converted them to text, and had AI write a job description for the journalist who'd write them. This job description is the prompt used to generate a new newsletter every Wednesday morning.

It'll never be the same, but it's something.

(Not affiliated with the original Dojo. Newsletters are generated by AI so check details before you go.)
